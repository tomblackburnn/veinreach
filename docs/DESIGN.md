# Design notes: the world of Veinreach

## Premise

Long ago a star refused to die. The ancients could not destroy it, so they sealed it beneath the world. Around the Seal they built **Obelisk Prime**, a thinking engine, and the **Warden's Keep**, a fortress whose tunnels spiral down toward the deep.

The Seal's slow leak of power shaped the land. Crystals sing in the Glimmer Hollows, the forest spirit rotted into the Thornwarden, and deep below, the Emberwyrm Nhal'Zyra sleeps in the fires the Seal keeps banked.

## Progression terminology

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The Seal** | The ancient binding at the heart of the world. |
| **The Unsealing** | The mid-game transformation that follows Obelisk Prime's destruction. |
| **Shardblight** | The violet crystal scar where the Seal broke. It creeps slowly outward. |
| **Void Essence** | Residue of the broken Seal, used for post-Unsealing crafting. |
| **Aurel** | The universal currency. |

## Ore ladder

Brasslite → Ferrocite → Moonsilver → Sungild → Glimmerite (Glimmer Hollows) → Cindrite (Emberdeep) → *(Unsealing)* → Umbralite → Aetherium → Starsteel (Starheart fragments from Solmara).

Pickaxe power gates each step: 35 / 45 / 55 / 62 / 75 / 100 / 120 / 160.

## Bosses

| # | Boss | Where / how | Mechanics | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Gravelmaw, the Burrowing Tyrant**: a house-sized mandibled beetle | Grubbling Lure, underground | Telegraphed charges (stuns on walls and drops ceiling rocks), leap shockwaves, rock volleys, burrow ambush with a dust trail; enraged phase 2 with grubling adds | Chitin gear, Smith NPC |
| 2 | **The Thornwarden**: a rotted forest-guardian mask | Withered Seed, surface at night | Thorn fans, telegraphed root spikes, splitting seed bombs, thornling adds; phase 2 spiral volleys and triple dashes; retreats at dawn | Heartwood gear, Explorer, halts the Blightmire's spread |
| 3 | **Obelisk Prime, Warden of the Seal**: a floating construct with orbiting shards | Resonant Prism, deep caverns | Shard rings, bullet spirals, homing orbs, sweeping laser → **overheat** window (defense 0); phase 2 shard-storm cage; phase 3 rotating cross-lasers | **The Unsealing**, Archmage, Aetherforge |
| 4 | **Nhal'Zyra, the Emberwyrm**: a 24-segment serpent | Brimstone Chalice, Emberdeep | Swims through terrain, orbit ember barrages, telegraphed sky dives, fire breath; phase 2 meteor rain | Starloom, the Astral Sigil |
| 5 | **Solmara, the Unmade Star**: final boss | Astral Sigil, night sky | Phase 1 star bursts, wisps, teleport dashes; phase 2 **arena ring**, rotating lasers, meteor columns; phase 3 supernova bullet-hell → **exhausted** window | Starsteel endgame gear |
