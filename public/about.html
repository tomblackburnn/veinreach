About Veinreach

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Veinreach is a free 2D sandbox adventure you play in the browser. Dig, build, craft and fight, on your own or with friends online.

Who makes it

Veinreach is made and run by Tom Blackburn, an individual based in the United Kingdom. It isn't a company, and the game has no purchases or adverts.

Contact

For questions, bug reports, reports about other players, or to ask for your data to be deleted, email asteroidthedino@gmail.com. You can also delete your account yourself from the Multiplayer menu; see the Privacy Policy.

Accessibility

We want as many people as possible to be able to play.

  • All menus work with a keyboard: Tab moves between controls, Enter or Space presses them, and Escape closes dialogs. The selected control is always highlighted.
  • Menu text meets the WCAG AA contrast level, and the game view, buttons and inputs have labels for screen readers.
  • You can change the interface size and turn off screen shake in Settings.

Known limits: playing the game itself needs a keyboard and mouse, and a screen reader can't describe the game world. If something stops you playing, please email asteroidthedino@gmail.com and we'll see what we can do.
